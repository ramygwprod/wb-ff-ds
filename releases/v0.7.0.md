# v0.7.0

**Date:** 2026-11-24, 21:33 EET (UTC+02:00)
**Goal achieved:** the colour foundation has its agent, its rules and its decisions. Colours are computed from a brand's inputs by rules that each state their reason, so the same rules serve any brand, both colour schemes and both conformance themes. No colour value is stored and no token file exists yet (see Known gaps).

## Added

### The colour agent

- **Manifest** `knowledge-base/foundations/color/agent.json`:
  - **Name:** `color`, the assignee for every colour task.
  - **Tools:** read, search, edit, and read-only web. No Bash.
  - **Write scope:** the colour `rules.json` and `sources.json` only.
  - **Finish gate:** `registry:check`, with two retries.

### Colour knowledge

- **Rules: 55**, in 10 groups: names and values; contrast and themes; surfaces, separators and shadows; text; accent and brand; status colours and chart series; states, focus and selection; gradients; code; forced colours. All are drafts. 46 are guidance and 9 are checks. Every rule states its reason.
- **Sources: 43.** Each states how far it was read: 35 read, 7 where only an abstract or a catalogue record was read, and 1 seen in metadata only.
- **Decisions: 120 → 213.** 173 are answered and current, 25 were replaced by a later decision and stay as history, 2 were set aside, and 13 are open. None of the open ones belongs to colour.

### What was decided

| Subject | Decisions | What they settle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How knowledge is held | DEC-158, DEC-159, DEC-160 | A foundation holds concepts, reasons and equations; values are computed. Tokens have three levels: scale, foundation, atom. A property a person sets has a range that WCAG limits |
| Where colour appears | DEC-161, DEC-197 | A colour appears only where it carries a meaning. One main action takes the accent colour. A brand colour is the tenant's identity: the system adapts around it and changes it only where WCAG leaves no other way |
| Brand inputs | DEC-165, DEC-213, DEC-147 | The accent colour is proposed from the logo. A logo and an image are used as provided and never recoloured; a variation under 3:1 is flagged for a person |
| Surfaces | DEC-162, DEC-163, DEC-181, DEC-164, DEC-166, DEC-125 | Four surface levels, the higher the brighter, in both colour schemes. The greys share one tint. No colour is pure black |
| Light | DEC-199, DEC-200, DEC-201, DEC-203, DEC-204, DEC-202 | One light gives every surface its gradient and its shadow. The AAA theme removes that detail. Shadows stay subtle. Texture comes from light only. A line is drawn only where WCAG 1.4.11 asks |
| Text | DEC-139, DEC-167, DEC-194, DEC-172 | Three levels of text in colour, met on every surface. On a fill, the text neutral with the higher contrast ratio |
| States and focus | DEC-168, DEC-179, DEC-169, DEC-170, DEC-171, DEC-176 | A state steps by one ratio. Disabled keeps 3:1. A selected item never takes the accent colour |
| Status colours | DEC-187, DEC-132, DEC-133, DEC-130, DEC-131, DEC-134, DEC-135, DEC-195, DEC-207 | A status is chosen by its meaning first, from the hues of official signal colours. Only four meanings take a status colour |
| Colour vision | DEC-185, DEC-192, DEC-175, DEC-178, DEC-190 | Meaningful colours stay 0.12 apart in Oklab for typical, red-blind, green-blind and blue-blind vision. A status hue moves at most 10 per cent of the way to the next status |
| Charts | DEC-136, DEC-196, DEC-206 | Series colours are proposed as colour harmonies from the accent colour, and a person chooses. On an ordered scale the high end is the full colour |
| Gradients | DEC-198, DEC-189, DEC-152, DEC-153 | A gradient is an option for different uses. On a surface it is the light on that surface |
| Schemes and themes | DEC-173, DEC-182, DEC-174, DEC-205, DEC-210, DEC-211 | Four colour scheme options. Day and night needs the user's permission. A change of colour scheme takes 2.5 s with text recomputed on every frame |
| Roles and properties | DEC-208, DEC-209, DEC-212, DEC-146, DEC-141, DEC-149 | Six CSS system colour roles and four CSS colour properties. Alpha only where what lies beneath is meant to show. Forced colours mode is respected everywhere |

Three decisions govern how the knowledge base is written:

- **DEC-180:** a rule carries its reason in a field of its own.
- **DEC-183:** the file of an agent holds its guidance rules and every rule they name.
- **DEC-191:** the contract version changes with every release that changes the contract.

Three older decisions were answered: DEC-029 (disabled), DEC-042 (a colour that fails in the AAA theme) and DEC-057 (where preferences live).

### The evidence behind the rules

- **Status hues.** Each is the mean of two official references, converted to OKLCH: the colour regions of US traffic signs and the colours of hazard labels.
- **Colour vision.** Every set of meaningful colours was measured as typical, red-blind, green-blind and blue-blind vision see it. The distance and the hue limit were each picked from a drawing after the measurement.
- **Text on a fill.** The two text neutrals are 20.25:1 apart, so every colour reaches 4.5:1 with one of them.
- **Light.** 1,152 settings of the light and 128 changes of colour scheme, 240 frames each, kept every text at its ratio on the test pages.
- **Read-back tests.** Before each batch was recorded, readers with no context answered set questions from the drafted rules: 207 of 210 answers were as intended over 19 rounds. For the last batch the questions were written from the owner's answers by a reader who had not seen the drafts.

## Changed

- **Registry contract: 0.1.0 → 0.2.0** (DEC-191). Every registry file states it, and the schemas carry it in their address.
- **Rules schema:** a rule may carry a `reason`. Once one rule in a file states its reason, every rule in that file must.
- **Registry check:**
  - A file where no rule states a reason is reported as a warning.
  - **New:** the check reads the rule and decision IDs inside a rule's text and reason. It fails where a rule named there does not exist, where a decision named there is missing from the rule's own list, and where such a decision does not exist, was replaced or was set aside.
- **Generator:** a rules table shows the reason. An agent's file gives each rule with its reason, and lists in full every rule those rules name.
- **Schema** `common.schema.json`: the list of who uses a rule gained `color`.
- **Layout and typography rules:** the gap that waited on DEC-057 is closed. Nothing else in them changed.
- **`CLAUDE.md`:** section 4 says where the colour rules are and that no colour value or token is stored yet.
- **README:** status.
- **`package.json`:** version 0.7.0, for the workspace and the library.

## Known gaps

- **No agent has run live.** Claude Code loads agent files when a session starts. Live runs come after the foundation phase.
- **There is no token file yet.** Until the token contract exists (DEC-026, DEC-159), a chosen value lives in the decision that set it, and colours named in notes are test values, not a brand's.
- **86 of 96 check rules have no check yet:** 9 in colour, 47 in layout and all 30 in typography. They wait for the token and check stages.
- **Layout and typography rules do not state their reasons yet.** The check reports both files with a warning.
- **Eight colour sources were not read in full:** seven rest on an abstract or a catalogue record and one on its title and journal only. Twelve more were read through a summarising fetch. Each says so in its note.
- **The light's geometry and the timing of the change of colour scheme are recorded as decisions** (DEC-200, DEC-205) but belong to the elevation and motion foundations, which do not exist yet.
- **Every rule is a draft or a proposal.** Approval is this release's review.
- **13 decisions are open,** all in layout and typography.
- **Carried over:** the accepted advisory GHSA-g3hv-75wg-m249; library tests stay skipped until its first component; fallback fonts were measured on macOS only.
